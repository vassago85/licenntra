Why owners choose it

Three things change the day you turn it on.

Licentra is built around the parts of the work that cost you money: incomplete submissions, lost tickets, and the trail you cannot reproduce when someone asks.

01 · THROUGHPUT

Work arrives complete

The dealer sees the exact checklist for the request type they picked — renewal, change-of-ownership, dealer stock into their own name or a fleet, MIB, data fix. Weighbridge, COF, body builder, title holder, proxy ID — on the screen, with the rule that triggered it.

02 · CONTROL

Reviewers finish things

One queue per dealership, with a nominated primary reviewer so recurring customers land on the right desk. Anyone can cover when the primary is on leave — the handover shows up in the audit log as "covering for X".

03 · DEFENSIBILITY

You can prove the work

Every stage change, every document decision, every hand-over to the customer is appended to a tamper-resistant audit log. Identity downloads are a separate permission and are audited by user.

The work, start to finish

Six phases, seventeen stages, one owner at every step.

01 · CLIENT

Draft

Live checklist for the request type — renewal, COO, dealer stock, MIB, data fix — plus category, owner and finance.

02 · REVIEWER

Document review

Accept, reject with a reason, request changes. SRF, weighbridge, COF, body builder, Original NaTIS warn-only on COO.

03 · YOU

Quote & payment

Snapshot the fees. The dealership's stock controller receives the invoice by default. Verified against snapshot on EFT or card-on-file.

04 · REVIEWER

Datafix

Confirm tare, body and GVM. Resolve client vs reviewer values before the authority sees it.

05 · YOU

Authority & disc

Record the authority reference, upload the returned NaTIS certificate and licence disc, close the job.

06 · CLIENT

Hand-over

Batched POD / POC per dealership. Digital confirm at the counter is the record of truth — a signed paper copy is optional. Or email the NaTIS and licence disc straight to the customer.

  • Live document rules per request, category, owner and financeEditable in the admin. Nothing hard-coded. Resolves per application and tracks per-document status. Original NaTIS is warn-only on change-of-ownership so the reviewer can decide
  • Vehicle data pre-fill for RLV / ALV formsFuel type, transmission, body description, drive type, usage, odometer, nature of ownership, reason for registration and 10+ other fields. Defaults from category (diesel for commercial, petrol for passenger) and request type

  • Stock controller as default invoice recipientThe client admin nominates one user per dealership from the Team page. Every quote and invoice goes to them by default. Overridable per transaction
  • Returned-documents workflowYour reviewer uploads the NaTIS certificate and licence disc when they come back from the authority. Dealers download them from the completed transaction

  • Datafix queue with client vs reviewer valuesTare, body, GVM reconciled before the authority sees it. Authority reference captured on confirmation
  • Fee tables per province, with draft and active versionsApprovers must be different from the creator. RTMC transaction fees, licence fees, per-licence vs per-transaction billing modes
  • Quotes, VAT, markup, payment capture and override reasonsSnapshotted onto the application when payment is requested. Account-level toggle controls who can accept a quote

  • Business-client register with retention and legal holdPOA max age, consent record, retention clock per client, secure deletion job with legal-hold override
  • Audit log that refuses updates and deletesEnforced by a database trigger, not just application code. Masks sensitive values. Identity downloads audited by user
  • Document scanning on uploadfinfo mime detection, 15 MB cap, SHA-256 hash. ClamAV container shipped (optional, flip on with CLAMAV_ENABLED)
  • Scheduler, queue worker and HorizonRedis-backed. Notifications: ready for collection, quote expiry, rejected document, hand-over confirmed. Mailgun transport with a settings-UI override
  • Licence cost estimatorStandalone tool for quick quotes. Province, category, tare / GVM. Driven by the same fee tables as live quotes

Not in scope

  • Direct NaTIS integrationYou enter the authority reference. We never claim to be eNaTIS
  • Card checkout or online payment gatewaysFinance verifies EFT or card-on-file against the snapshot
  • Multi-tenant on a shared databaseOne licensing company per deployment. One database. No tenant_id leaks
  • PDF overlay onto government formsWe render a review-and-edit screen in the browser. Your reviewer checks pre-filled values, edits inline, prints the pack
  • Marketing claims we cannot defendNo "POPIA compliant" badge. Controls are listed. You decide what to say

Deployment

One deployment per licensing company. Your brand, your database.

Licentra is shipped and operated by Charsley Digital. We stand it up for you, hand you the admin, and keep it running. You can run it on your infrastructure or on ours. Hosted in South Africa.

Platform: Laravel 13, Livewire 4, Filament 5.9, PHP 8.4
Runtime: Nginx + PHP-FPM in Docker, Supervisor-managed
Data: PostgreSQL 16, Redis 7, local or S3-compatible object storage
Hosting: Docker on your VM, or Charsley-managed in SA
Auth: Fortify, 2FA, passkeys, Spatie permissions, absolute session lifetime
Files: Private filesystem on a dedicated storage volume. SHA-256 hashed, optional ClamAV scan on upload
Audit: Append-only, enforced by a database trigger. Identity downloads audited by user
Mail: Mailgun transport with a Site Settings UI override
Branding: Logo, primary colour, reference prefix, support address and phone
Retention: Per business client, with legal-hold override and secure-deletion job
Scope: No NaTIS integration, no card gateway, no shared tenants
